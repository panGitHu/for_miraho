# About Miraho

We are **Miraho**, a kitchenware and restaurant equipment company based in **Guangzhou, Guangdong Province, China**.

We specialize in the production and sales of commercial kitchen and catering equipment, offering a complete end-to-end service that covers everything from initial consultation and sales through to professional installation. Whether you are outfitting a new restaurant, upgrading a central kitchen, or scaling a chain operation, we provide the equipment, the expertise, and the hands-on support to get it running.

## What Sets Us Apart

- **In-house manufacturing** — we operate our own dedicated factory, giving us direct control over quality, lead times, and customization.
- **A complete company, not a trader** — with our own production floor and a specialized sales team working side by side, we cover the full chain under one roof: manufacturing → sales → delivery → installation.
- **One-stop service** — from product selection and specification to on-site installation and after-sales support, a single partner handles the entire process.
- **Built for foodservice professionals** — our products are engineered for the demands of real commercial kitchens: durability, efficiency, and reliable daily operation.

## Our Service Chain

1. **Consultation & Sales** — our sales team helps you spec the right equipment for your menu, layout, and capacity.
2. **Production** — manufactured in our own factory under controlled quality standards.
3. **Delivery & Installation** — professional installation team sets up and commissions your equipment on site.
4. **After-sales Support** — ongoing maintenance and technical support to keep your kitchen running.

## Location

Guangzhou, Guangdong Province, China — at the heart of the Greater Bay Area, one of the world's most active manufacturing and export hubs. This gives us access to a mature supply chain, fast logistics, and the capacity to serve both domestic and overseas customers efficiently.
